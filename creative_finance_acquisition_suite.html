Quick guide to the structures

Seller financing

The seller finances some or all of the purchase price. You negotiate the down payment, interest rate, payment schedule, maturity, default terms, and security instrument. Confirm lien priority and whether the seller has an existing mortgage.

Subject-to existing financing

You acquire the property while the seller's existing mortgage generally remains in the seller's name. The buyer may make the payments, but the seller can remain liable to the lender. Review due-on-sale provisions, insurance, servicing, title, and legal protections with qualified professionals.

Balloon payment

A balloon is a lump-sum balance due at a set maturity date, even if the monthly payments are calculated over a longer amortization period. A property may cash flow and still fail if the balloon cannot be paid.

How to interpret the rental metrics

  • NOI: rent after vacancy and modeled operating costs, before debt service.
  • Cash flow: NOI minus modeled debt payment.
  • Cash-on-cash: annual cash flow divided by modeled cash needed at closing.
  • DSCR: NOI divided by annual debt service. Lenders may calculate this differently.
  • Gross rent / price: monthly rent divided by purchase price. It is a rough screen, not a profitability test.
